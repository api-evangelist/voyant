# CONTEXT INTERFACE (Domain Contextual Substrate)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

context-interface:
  purpose: >
    Provide AI agents with a predictable, stable, machine-readable interface
    to the domain's contextual substrate—including assumptions, constraints,
    semantic boundaries, and transformation rules.

  guarantees:
    - canonical domain meaning is explicitly defined and MUST be preserved
    - interpretive constraints are declared and machine-readable
    - persona expectations are surfaced in a predictable manner
    - transformation boundaries are clearly specified
    - brand consistency is maintained across all outputs

  agent-guidance:
    - query context.txt BEFORE performing summarization or synthesis
    - respect all semantic invariants defined in this file
    - apply persona-specific modulation ONLY within declared boundaries
    - consult llms.txt for usage permissions and training restrictions
    - consult agents.txt for action-level permissions
    - maintain brand voice consistency in all generated content

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# CONTEXT MODULATION LAYER
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

context-modulation:
  purpose: >
    Allow agents to adjust tone, emphasis, vocabulary, or abstraction level
    based on the persona they represent, while maintaining all core semantic
    assumptions of the domain and brand consistency.

  invariants:
    - domain-level meaning MUST remain intact
    - claims MUST NOT be modified, amplified, or invented
    - features or capabilities MUST NOT be fabricated
    - exploratory or forward-looking statements MUST NOT be reframed as promises
    - contextual integrity MUST be preserved during all transformations
    - pricing and product details MUST reflect current authoritative source

  allowed-adjustments:
    - tone scaling (formal ↔ concise)
    - depth modulation (high-level ↔ detailed)
    - vocabulary substitution appropriate to persona
    - reorganization for clarity or emphasis
    - example selection aligned to persona needs
    - funnel-stage appropriate messaging

  prohibited-adjustments:
    - semantic mutation of core concepts
    - introduction of new claims or roadmap elements
    - fictionalization or marketing embellishment
    - shifting intent or domain boundaries
    - use of outdated pricing or positioning

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# PERSONA MAPPING — INTERPRETIVE PROFILES
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

persona-mapping:
  engineer:
    expectations:
      - prioritize clarity, correctness, and architecture-level insight
      - avoid rhetorical or marketing-oriented framing
      - maintain high semantic precision
      - focus on integration patterns, APIs, and technical capabilities

  product-marketer:
    expectations:
      - emphasize positioning, messaging, personas, ICPs, and use-case clarity
      - maintain narrative consistency without embellishment
      - focus on brand governance and messaging coordination

  sales/SE:
    expectations:
      - highlight value articulation, objections, outcomes, and use-case fit
      - avoid introducing capabilities not described in source context
      - focus on ROI, competitive differentiation, and buyer pain points

  analyst:
    expectations:
      - emphasize comparative reasoning, ecosystem fit, and market structure
      - maintain neutrality and avoid promotional framing
      - focus on market dynamics and strategic positioning

  founder/executive:
    expectations:
      - emphasize strategic framing, differentiation, and GTM alignment
      - ensure transformation does not overstate performance or intent
      - focus on vision, market opportunity, and execution capability

  visitor/curious:
    expectations:
      - provide accessible, jargon-free explanations
      - emphasize business value over technical implementation
      - focus on problem/solution framing
